FAQ

Common questions

Is the app really free?

Yes — completely free. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions. Ever.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All puzzles are generated on your device. You never need an internet connection to play.

Will I run out of puzzles?

No. Puzzles are algorithmically generated — Sudoku, Word Search, Nonograms and most other games produce unlimited unique puzzles automatically.

My progress disappeared after an update

Progress is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app will erase your progress. We recommend not uninstalling if you want to preserve your streaks. If this happened after an update, please contact us — we want to know.

A game is not working correctly

Please email us with the game name, what happened, and your device model and iOS version. We take bugs seriously and aim to fix them fast.

Can I suggest a new puzzle game?

Absolutely — we love ideas. Send your suggestion to our support email below.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings inside the app → scroll to the bottom → tap "Reset All Progress". This cannot be undone.
